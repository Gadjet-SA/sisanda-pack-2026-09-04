## What's new (seller-facing, last ~10 days)

### A. Bring returns home from the DC (3–4 Sep)

**What it is.** Gadjet books a courier that starts at a Takealot returns facility and delivers to the seller warehouse. Waybill PDF and live tracking. Removal orders at the same DC share one collection and one waybill.

**How to use it.** Store → Returns → Pickup ready. Tick the rows (first tick locks the DC). Book collection. Or in chat: *“ship my returns back from JHB”*. After booking, paste that waybill on Takealot Seller Portal → Removal Orders. Gadjet books the courier; it does not submit the Takealot pickup.

**Then vs now.** Then: portal only, wait for TCS. Now: Gadjet courier, waybill in the app, several ROs on one trip if they sit at the same DC.

### B. The repricer names who you're fighting (4 Sep)

**What it is.** Automations → Repricer → SKUs shows the listing TSIN and a Competitor column (storefront, price, rating). Open a SKU and you get every seller on that listing, plus who holds the buy box.

**Then vs now.** Then: “3 competitors”. Now: “Photo24 at R189, 4.6★, buy box”.

### C. Stock value, split the way a seller thinks (31 Aug / 2 Sep)

**What it is.** Overview Stock value splits Takealot vs warehouse, JHB / CPT / DBN, and selling (incl. VAT) vs cost (excl. VAT).

**Then vs now.** Then: one headline. Now: two pools, three DCs, selling and cost.

### D. Listing images — Gemini 3.1, square lifestyle (2–3 Sep)

**What it is.** Listing Image Studio renders on Gemini 3.1 Flash Image. Lifestyle shots are requested at 1:1.

**How to use it.** In chat: *“make lifestyle images for SKU X”*. Starter includes 10 images/month, then R1.96/image.

### E. Empty chat got out of the way (4 Sep)

**What it is.** A new chat is a large greeting and four short chips above the composer.

**Then vs now.** Then: onboarding lecture. Now: “What do you want to do?” and four chips.

### F. Promo approvals on a phone (1 Sep)

The promotion line table is usable in a vertical viewport — approve or reject without pinching a desktop grid.
